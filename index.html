The idea

Bonjour, Multan.

Bonva showed up in Model Town with a simple idea: café culture doesn't need a plane ticket. Flaky croissants, loaded sandwiches, Dubai-style mini burgers, a full coffee bar — and a room built for lingering, not just eating.

Regulars mention the cozy, warmly lit space before anything else — a small book collection on the wall, edison-bulb sconces, and staff who'll ask how you take your coffee before they make it.

The menu runs deep — a dozen-plus coffees hot and iced, teas, smoothies, mains, and a rotating seasonal drop (mango season, we see you) — priced reasonably, because a café open from noon to 2AM has to work for study sessions and late-night catch-ups in equal measure.
